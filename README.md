## 📂 Projects

### 🎬 [IMDb Movie Database Design & Analysis](./projects/imdb-movie-database) — *MGS 613, Database Management Systems*

Designed and normalized a relational database (3NF) from the Kaggle "IMDb Top 1000" dataset, then wrote SQL to answer business questions a streaming service might ask.

- Built an ER diagram and relational schema modeling Movies, Directors, Actors, and Genres, including many-to-many associative entities

- Wrote DDL and cleaning rules to bring a denormalized 16-column CSV into a proper multi-table schema

- Authored 10 analytical SQL queries — top-grossing directors/actors/genres, ratings vs. genre averages, high Metascore directors — each tied back to a streaming-catalog recommendation

- **Team project** with Herman Von Hassel, John Kernin, Quan Le, and Xinyu Zheng

- **Tools:** Oracle APEX, SQL

### 📊 [ABC Mart: Testing Claims About Population Parameters](./projects/abcmart-hypothesis-testing) — *Statistical Analysis*

Used one-sample t-tests to check two management claims against data from 200 stores.

- Tested whether mean monthly profit exceeds $23.5k and whether mean service quality score is below 4, at α = 0.01 and α = 0.10

- Computed t-statistics and p-values in Python (NumPy, SciPy, Pandas), then wrote a managerial report translating the stats into a business recommendation

- Found no evidence profit exceeds the $23.5k benchmark, but strong evidence (p ≈ 0.000004) that service quality falls short of a 4.0 target — recommended a service quality improvement initiative over relying on the profit benchmark

- **Team project** with Sumaia Hoque, Stephon Vergara, and Andrew Jung

- **Tools:** Python, Jupyter Notebook, Microsoft Word
